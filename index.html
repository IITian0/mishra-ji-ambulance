FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do you provide ambulance service 24×7? +
Yes. We are available 24 hours a day, all 7 days a week, including holidays.
How fast can an ambulance reach me? +
We aim for the fastest possible response. As soon as you call with your location, we dispatch the nearest available ambulance.
What medical equipment is available? +
Depending on the ambulance chosen — oxygen, first-aid kit, ventilator, cardiac monitor, defibrillator, infusion pump and trained attendants.
Do you do out-of-city / all-India transport? +
Yes. We offer intercity and interstate patient transport across India, including air and train ambulance assistance.
How are the charges decided? +
Charges depend on the type of ambulance and the distance. We keep pricing transparent and fair with no hidden charges. Call us for a quick estimate.
